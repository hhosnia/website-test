Three days, one arc.

Each day builds on the one before. The schedule below is a draft and will change as speakers confirm.

What's possible

Look ahead together. AI leaders show the frontier. Business leaders describe the problems they'd most like solved.

  • Morning
    Opening conversationA business leader and an AI leader open with where each sees the biggest opportunity.
  • Midday
    The frontier, plainly explainedResearchers and builders explain what's new, without jargon.
  • Afternoon
    Problems worth solvingBusiness leaders pitch real challenges to a room of AI experts.

What's available right now

Come down to earth. Real deployments, real budgets, and an honest look at what works today.

  • Morning
    Case studies from the fieldLeaders who shipped AI into their business share results, costs and mistakes.
  • Midday
    Hands-on tool sessionsSmall groups try current tools on their own problems.
  • Afternoon
    Ask the expertsOpen Q&A on risk, data, hiring, and build versus buy.

How to leverage each other

Turn conversations into plans. Pair up, scope projects, and agree on what happens next.

  • Morning
    Working pairsEach business leader works with an AI leader to sketch a concrete project.
  • Midday
    Share-outsPairs present what they found, and the room gives feedback.
  • Afternoon
    CommitmentsEveryone leaves with a next step, a named partner, and a date.

Two kinds of leaders. Equal footing.

Business leaders

  • Founders, executives and operators
  • People who own a real problem and a real budget
  • Leaders who want to know what they can do now

AI leaders

  • Researchers, engineers and product builders
  • People shipping AI into production
  • Leaders who want to learn where it's needed most
The overlap is the point: everyone teaches, and everyone learns.
